# Privacy Policy for Last Flight Out

_Last updated: October 6, 2026_

Last Flight Out ("the game") is a mobile game for Android. This policy explains what information the game and the ads in it use.
If anything here is unclear, contact: **sparrow.exile800@passmail.net**

## What we collect
**We do not collect personal information ourselves.** The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, and no servers of our own.

The game stores two things **only on your device**: your best distance and your sound setting. This data never leaves your phone and is deleted if you uninstall the game.

## Ads (Google AdMob)
The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as:
- your device's advertising ID,
- your IP address and approximate location,
- device and app information (model, operating system, app version),
- how you interact with ads.

This is done by Google, not by us. See how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
and Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

**Your choices**
- In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice any time from **Privacy Options** on the game's main menu (shown where required).
- On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Settings > Privacy > Ads.

## Children
The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at the same address.
